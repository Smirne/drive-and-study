EventStorming drill

Questions on the ideas in Alberto Brandolini's Introducing EventStorming, to prepare for facilitating a workshop. Read and tap in silence, or have them read aloud and answer by voice or with the car's steering-wheel buttons. The app explains each answer and remembers what you missed.

Facilitation scenarios & readiness check

Twelve workshop situations where every answer earns 5, 3, 1 or 0 points, with the reasons after each one. Then a 20-question readiness check with no feedback until the end.
